Alchemical components:

Dust
The only matter that is not defined in anyway by soul. It just is and reacts to other matter in accordance to rules of physics.

Mold
Substance that is entirely defined by soul. As mold it has litle to no apparent attributes other than it can be defined to become any other matter through "molding" The process is unrevercible so Mold is increasingly rare. It was first introduced to Eno in the meteor on which the children arrived on.

Mucus
A living subtance that has a ability to transform any substance throug a process of transformation. It mutates, and evolves living things and unliving things it transform either by making them more complex substances of simplifying them through erosion, oxydacion and corrosion.

Ash
Matter after the soul has left. A remainder, ghost-trace of what once was.
Always brittle, inert but strangely “echoing” with the memory of forms. 
Represents _finality_ — neither Dust nor Mold, but the husk left behind.

Frost (or “Rime”)
Soul that has frozen and become crystalline. Holds its definition stubbornly, like a stasis field. It preserves but also prevents growth. Anti-Mucus. Instead of transformation, it enforces stillness.

Sap
Soul condensed into viscous liquid that binds matter together. Acts like an adhesive of identity. When added to Dust or Mold, it creates continuity — like memory or history. Repairs missing. Makes whole.

Glow (or “Phos”)
Soul matter that exists only as radiation, never settling into form. Seen as light, warmth, inspiration, or even madness depending on exposure. Represents the raw energy that animates Dust, Mold, and Mucus into dynamics.

Pitch
Inverse of Glow. Where Glow is anything, Pitch is pure negation: the substance that consumes definitions. Properties: Wherever it spreads, it erases distinctions between Dust, Mold, or Mucus, reducing them to “blank.” It’s not destruction — it’s un-definition. The Black hole within Eno. Most who know of it fear it, some use it ritually as “forgetting” or purification.


- **1. Dust** – foundation, matter, body
    
- **2. Mold** – potential, soul, becoming
    
- **3. Mucus** – transformation, decay, growth
    
- **4. Rime** – preservation, silence, stillness
    
- **5. Phos** – dream, vision, inspiration
    
- **6. Pitch** – void, forgetting, negation
    
- **7. Sap** – renewal, healing, memory
    
- **8. Ash** – death, residue, loss

- **Grave Lead** – Plon + Ash → heavy cursed ingots that attract wandering souls.
    
- **Dreamfire** – Suhra + Phos → luminous, unstable burning powder.
    
- **Soulstone** – Charon + Mold → black crystal that records memories.

## 🔹 Cultural Layers

- **High Alchemists (Crafters):** Work directly with Dust, Mold, Phos, etc. Dangerous but powerful.
    
- **Low Alchemists (Artificers):** Mostly manipulate natural elements, adding traces of Crafters to create usable “stuffs.”
    
- **Guild Rivalries:** Some guilds see Sap vs. Ash as the true polarity (life ↔ death), others swear by Dust vs. Mold (matter ↔ soul).


Origins of alchemical elements:

- **1. Dust** – Concept of Dust was imagined by Omi as a matter that is only defined by its relations to other material things. It is now found as a component in most things that need to resist ideatic manipulation.
    
- **2. Mold** – The material of the meteor that landed on Eno 10 000 cycles ago. Home was constructed using it and it has become more and more rare since.
    
- **3. Mucus** – Norians have a gland in their brain that contains a minute amount of Mucus in it. It activates during puberty and it sets in motion the small scale mutation that differentiates the different generations of norians from one another. There have been trials to create synthetic mucus with harvesting these glands and trying to separate the virus from them. So far this has proven difficult. Indused mutation and accelerated evolution holds alot of promise to many alchemists.
    
- **4. Rime** – Souls that make the pilgrimage to O and stand in the precense of the moon at the dark side of Eno leave in their place a trace amounts of Rime. This is collected by constructs that can withstand the extreme conditions of the Eternal night. Rime holds the perfect schema of the being that it once was. Usually they are reluctant to be awakened.
    
- **5. Phos** – Nula made the Concept of Phos  as the ether of which a soulsphere is filled. It is gaseus so it needs a container or else it dissipates . It contains the soul as ethereal glow that is not defined by matter. 
    
- **6. Pitch** – Found in the in between area of Eno where O or A do not have influence and Eno has a weakened hold. It flows in the cracks of a great mountain of tempest and some of it can end up in the depths of everchanging sea. It absords soul if it becomes in contact with it and is very frickle thing to tamper with. Some roamers that harvest the depths can posses this matter for trade and otherwise.
    
- **7. Sap** – The rooted generate sap within their bodies and without it they perish. Sap can be harvested but with great care and the life force extracted should be replaced with a substitute if it is practised substainably. Usually a sacrificial living beings are used to replenish the harvested sap. It rejuvinates and heals and makes whole that that was once broken. 
    
- **8. Ash** –  Souls that make the pilgrimage to A and stand in the precense of the sun at the light side of Eno leave in their place a trace amounts of Ash. This is collected by constructs that can withstand the extreme conditions of the Eternal Day. Ash holds remnant traces of the being that it once was part of. Ash consists of the unclean part of being that was shed to purify the soul. Usually they long to be connected with the soul they once were part of.
